## How these numbers were derived

- **Balance** = `ar_invoice.amount_due` − payments − discounts applied from `ar_cash_invoice`. Foundation does **not** decrement `amount_due` as cash comes in, so balance must be computed. Verified: all **3,197** closed invoices compute to exactly $0.00.
- **Excluded**: reversals (`reversal='Y'`), unposted, and non-active records. Note 635 invoices still flagged open are in fact fully paid — they're excluded by the computed balance, not by `closed_flag`.
- **Aging** = `DATEDIFF(day, due_date, 2026-08-26) > 60` — measured from **due date**, not invoice date. Lowest value in the result set is 62 days, confirming nothing at 60 or under leaked in.
- **`ar_history` was deliberately not summed** — its 4,249 cash rows are an exact mirror of `ar_cash_invoice` (identical $132,261,132.84); adding both would double-count every payment.
- **Cross-checked** two independent ways (CTE join vs. correlated subquery) — both return 177 invoices / $574,821.29.
- Read-only throughout; nothing in Foundation was modified.
